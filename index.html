What we fixed

In the first version, voice D sometimes read the punctuation aloud. It said “punto” at the end of sentences (for example “…la tua guida per la sicurezza punto…” or “…digestori anaerobici punto punto…”), and once it repeated a phrase (“punto di raccolta punto di raccolta”).

The cause: the voice engine sometimes treats the full stop at the end of a sentence as a word to pronounce. What we changed:

  • Each sentence is now spoken on its own, without the final full stop, and the sentences are joined with a short natural pause. Colons become short pauses. “!” and “?” stay, because they give the right tone.
  • Steadier settings, so the voice no longer repeats words.
  • Each line is recorded four times. Automatic speech recognition checks every take, and the clearest one is kept. A take that says “punto” or “virgola” where the text does not is never used.
