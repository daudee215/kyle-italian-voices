What we fixed this time

Removing the final full stop stopped the spoken “punto”, but it made the voice end each sentence too early: the last word was rushed or cut (“esci”, “anaerobici”), and “Buongiorno!” was spoken alone, which this voice engine does badly with very short phrases.

  • Short phrases are no longer spoken alone. “Buongiorno!” now flows into the rest of the sentence, as a person would say it.
  • Three sentence endings tested on every line (none, full stop, “…”). The full stop brought “punto” back in one take out of three, so those takes were thrown away; the soft “…” never did and gave the clearest last words.
  • Some takes also listened to Italian samples of the same voice, for a more natural Italian accent.
  • Every take was checked word by word, including the last word of each sentence. For each line the best of 18 takes was kept.
  • A slightly longer silence after each line, so the end is not clipped.
